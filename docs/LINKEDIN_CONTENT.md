## Caption — Professional version

Just wrapped up a new Power BI project: Sales Performance & Revenue Growth Dashboard.

I built this to simulate how sales analyst and revenue management teams actually track performance — not just total revenue, but whether targets are being hit, which regions and categories are actually profitable, and how growth is trending month over month.

What went into it:
→ Cleaned and transformed 150+ transaction records in Power Query (nulls, duplicates, inconsistent formatting)
→ Built 10+ DAX measures — Achievement %, Growth %, Category-wise Performance, Sales Executive Performance
→ Designed a dashboard with KPI cards, a Target-vs-Actual comparison chart, a monthly revenue trend line, and region/category breakdowns

The most useful insight: the highest-revenue region and the region with the best target achievement weren't the same — a reminder that hitting your number matters as much as the size of your number.

## How to post it

1. **Images to upload:** the full dashboard screenshot first, followed by 2–3 close-ups — the KPI card row, the Target-vs-Actual chart, and the monthly revenue trend line.
2. **First line matters most:** LinkedIn truncates after 2–3 lines, so the hook line needs to stand on its own before "see more."
3. **GitHub link placement:** put it in the first comment, not the post body — outbound links in the post body get suppressed by the algorithm. Mention "link in comments" at the end of the caption.
4. **Credibility signals:** name the tools explicitly (Power BI, Power Query, DAX) rather than saying "data tools."
5. **Tag relevant communities/pages sparingly** — don't tag individuals who didn't contribute.
