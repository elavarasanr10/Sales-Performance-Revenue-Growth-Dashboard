# Project Overview

## What this project is

A Power BI dashboard that tracks sales performance and revenue growth at transaction level: units sold, revenue, cost, profit, and target achievement — sliced by region, product category, sales executive, channel, and customer type.

## Why sales performance tracking and revenue growth analysis matter

Sales activity without a target-and-growth lens hides the real story:

- **Revenue tells you volume; Achievement % tells you whether it's enough.** A region can post strong revenue and still be badly missing its target.
- **Growth % catches direction, not just level.** A region with lower revenue but strong month-over-month growth may be a better bet for future investment than a flat high-revenue region.
- **Category and executive breakdowns guide coaching and stocking decisions.** Knowing which product category or sales executive consistently over- or under-performs changes where a company focuses training and inventory.
- **Channel and customer-type splits guide marketing spend.** If Online + New Customer transactions convert at a higher rate than any other combination, that's where acquisition budget should go next.

## How companies use dashboards like this one

Sales and revenue teams typically review a dashboard like this weekly or monthly: comparing actuals to target, checking which regions or executives need support, and flagging categories with declining growth before it shows up in the quarterly numbers. It becomes the shared reference in sales review meetings and leadership reporting.

## Job roles that use this kind of tool

- **Sales Analyst** — monitors revenue, target achievement, and trend data
- **Business Analyst** — builds and maintains the reporting layer for sales stakeholders
- **Revenue Manager** — owns target-setting and achievement tracking
- **Sales Manager** — uses executive- and region-level breakdowns to coach the team
- **Regional/Category Manager** — tracks their own region or category's contribution to growth

## How MBA and business-analytics students benefit

This project gives a portfolio piece that speaks the language recruiters in sales analytics and revenue operations roles expect: target vs. actual, achievement %, growth %, and channel-wise performance — not just a generic "sales dashboard." It also forces practice with the full BI workflow (data cleaning → modeling → DAX → storytelling), which is what's actually tested in BA and sales analyst interviews.
