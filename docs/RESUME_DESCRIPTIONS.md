# Resume-Ready Project Descriptions

## One-line version

Built a Power BI dashboard tracking sales performance, revenue growth, and target achievement across regions and product categories.

## 2–3 line version

Designed and built a Sales Performance & Revenue Growth dashboard in Power BI, covering 150+ transactions across regions, product categories, sales executives, and channels. Used Power Query for data cleaning and DAX for KPIs including Achievement %, Growth %, and Category-wise Performance. Delivered region-, category-, and executive-level breakdowns to identify where the business is over- or under-performing target.

## Strong ATS-friendly professional version

Developed an end-to-end Sales Performance & Revenue Growth dashboard using Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to track revenue, cost, profit, and target achievement across regions, product categories, sales channels, and sales executives. Cleaned and transformed raw transactional data (handling nulls, duplicates, and inconsistent formatting) into an analysis-ready model. Built 10+ DAX measures — including Achievement %, Growth %, and Category-wise Performance — and a multi-visual dashboard (KPI cards, target-vs-actual comparison, monthly trend line, region/category breakdowns) enabling stakeholders to evaluate sales performance and prioritize growth strategy.

Sales Performance & Revenue Growth Dashboard — a Power BI project simulating how sales analyst and revenue management teams track target achievement and growth across regions and product categories.
